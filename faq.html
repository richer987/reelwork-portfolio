UGC (user-generated content) style video is footage that looks and feels like it was filmed by a real customer or creator, rather than a polished studio ad. It typically performs better on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and in paid social because audiences trust it more than traditional advertising.

Standard turnaround is 3–5 business days from final brief to delivered files, depending on your package. Rush delivery (24–48hr) is available as an add-on for an extra fee.

For physical products, you'll ship samples to our creators before filming begins. For digital products or apps, we can usually film directly from screen recordings and a demo account you provide.

You own full rights to organic posting on your own social channels with every package. Paid advertising usage rights are included in the Growth and Pro packages, or available as an add-on on Starter.

Starter includes 1 revision round per video, Growth includes 2, and Pro includes unlimited revisions. Revisions cover edits like pacing, captions, and music — not full reshoots.
